❓ Help — quick answers

        

          
🧾 Book entries

          
Add a book entry — sale or purchase, party, amount, GST. Every entry is posted to a double-entry group exactly once; large or unusual entries land in Pending approvals for your yes/no.

        

        

          
📦 Customer orders

          
Place them from the dashboard or let customers send them to your linked WhatsApp — typed text, a voice note, or a photo of a handwritten list all become real orders with quantity and unit. Voice/photo paths need ASR/OCR keys on the server (GEMINI_API_KEY / VOSK_ENDPOINT) — until then customers are asked to type.

        

        

          
🎯 Offers

          
When making a bill, pick % percent off or ₹ cash off and type the value — the discount shows in the Offer column and reduces the total automatically.

        

        

          
💸 Payments

          
UPI orders confirm themselves the moment the payment link is generated. Cash orders wait for you to tap 💵 Confirm cash in the orders table — nothing is counted until you do.

        

        

          
🔗 Channels

          
Link Telegram (for login codes) with the /start code, and WhatsApp via first contact. Server keys live in the env file, never in this page — see .env.example.

        

        

          
🌐 Language

          
The selector in the topbar sets the language this dashboard and your agent's messages use. It saves instantly.

        

        

          
🏦 Loan pre-check

          
Score your books against an OCEN-style pre-screen before applying — soft check, no credit impact.

        

        
Stuck on something not covered here? Message the shop's linked Telegram chat — the agent answers in your preferred language.
